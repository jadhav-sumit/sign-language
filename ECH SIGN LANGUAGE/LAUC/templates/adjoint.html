What is Matrix Adjoint?

The adjoint of a square matrix is the transpose of its cofactor matrix. It's used to find the inverse of a matrix:

A⁻¹ = (1/det(A)) × adj(A)

Calculation Steps:

  1. Calculate the determinant of the matrix
  2. Find the cofactor matrix (Cij = (-1)i+j × Mij)
  3. Transpose the cofactor matrix to get the adjoint
  4. Divide adjoint by determinant to get the inverse
